## Must read

- General Path: Necrosis (esp. coagulative/caseous), acute inflammation events, wound healing, benign vs malignant, metastasis.
- Hematology: Iron deficiency anaemia (lab dx), megaloblastic anaemia (lab dx), AML (FAB), CML.
- Bone/Tumours: Osteosarcoma, ameloblastoma.
- Clinical: Thrombosis, shock, fracture healing.

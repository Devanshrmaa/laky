## Must read

- General: Pharmacokinetics (bioavailability), pharmacodynamics (antagonism).
- CNS: Local anaesthetics (lignocaine) — most important, NSAIDs (aspirin), antiepileptics (phenytoin).
- Chemotherapy: Penicillins (amoxicillin), rifampicin (TB), metronidazole, fluconazole.
- Dental: Dentifrices, mummifying agents, styptics, candidiasis treatment, ANUG treatment.
- Systemic: Adrenaline, beta blockers, ACE inhibitors, diuretics, insulin/metformin, corticosteroids.
- GIT: Peptic ulcer drugs (PPIs / H2 blockers).
